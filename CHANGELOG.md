# Changelog

The version shows at the bottom of the Settings screen in the app.

## 1.7.2 · Oct 16, 2026
- One Settings switch, Tap assist, now turns all the fat-finger help on or off: the glow under your finger and ignoring taps between two arrows
- Fixed: sliding your finger away to cancel a tap could make the very next tap do nothing

## 1.7.1 · Oct 15, 2026
- The date beside the version in Settings now updates with every release (it was stuck on Oct 7)

## 1.7.0 · Oct 15, 2026
- Taps go to the arrow whose drawn line is nearest your finger
- A tap right between two arrows does nothing and doesn't count as a miss; both arrows blink so you can tap again (Settings → Ignore unclear taps)
- A soft glow shows which arrow is under your finger; slide away before lifting to cancel

## 1.6.1 · Oct 13, 2026
- A strip below the board catches near-misses and sends them to the nearest arrow instead of the buttons
- Restart, Hint and Settings ignore taps that land right after an arrow tap
- Restart asks for a second tap while a board is in progress

## 1.6.0 · Oct 13, 2026
- Sort the best-times list by level, time, hints, misses, or Overall, which blends all three. Tap the active sort again to reverse it

## 1.5.1 · Oct 12, 2026
- Fixed: on narrow screens (Display Zoom, iPhone mini and SE) the hint squares made the page wider than the screen and cut off the right edge
- Narrow screens get tighter button spacing, and at the narrowest the button icons are dropped so the labels fit

## 1.5.0 · Oct 12, 2026
- Best times: your fastest clear of every level is saved per difficulty, shown beside the hearts while you play, and listed in Stats, where tapping a level replays it
- The win card says whether you beat your best, and after a replay offers a quick way back to your furthest level
- Hints are limited per board: Standard 2, Expert 3, Casual and Relaxed mode unlimited. Squares on the Hint button show what's left
- A hint now stays lit until you use it, and brings the arrow into view when you're zoomed in

## 1.4.0 · Oct 7, 2026
- New picture boards: lion, penguin, owl, turtle, butterfly, nested stars, sun, paw print, crown, snowflake, duck, fish and apple
- Cat and house now have eyes, a nose, windows and a door
- Removed the rocket, mushroom and ring shapes

## 1.3.1 · Oct 7, 2026
- Fit board moved next to the timer, so it no longer covers arrows

## 1.3.0 · Oct 7, 2026
- Pinch and drag on the board work like iOS Photos: zoom follows your fingers, edges stretch and spring back, flings glide to a stop
- Fixed: a double tap outside the board could zoom the whole page, and pinching couldn't undo it
- Faster redraws while zooming on big boards

## 1.2.1 · Oct 7, 2026
- Fixed: on iPhone the board could be drawn larger than its frame, cutting off outer rows and columns
- The arrow that blocks you stays highlighted longer, so it's easier to spot

## 1.2.0 · Oct 7, 2026
- Settings → Go to level accepts any level number, including levels you haven't played yet

## 1.1.1 · Oct 7, 2026
- The timer starts on your first arrow tap instead of when the board appears

## 1.1.0 · Oct 7, 2026
- Version number shown at the bottom of Settings

## 1.0.0 · Oct 7, 2026
- First home-screen release: app icon, offline play, full-screen launch
- Daily puzzle with streaks, timer and stats screen
- Casual, Standard and Expert difficulty
- Dense boards with shape silhouettes, pinch to zoom
- Blocked arrows turn red and leave on their own once their blocker is gone
