Settings

Difficulty
Casual is smaller and gentler. Expert is bigger and starts with only a few arrows free to tap. Hints per board: Casual unlimited, Standard 2, Expert 3. The current board restarts.
Relaxed mode
No hearts and unlimited hints. Blocked arrows still turn red and wait their turn.
Tap assist
Glows the arrow under your finger, and ignores a tap that lands right between two arrows so it can't cost you a miss. Turn off for plain taps.
Sound
Soft whoosh on release, thud on a block.
