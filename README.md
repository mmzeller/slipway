# Slipway

An ad-free arrow puzzle. Tap an arrow and it slides off the board along its line, but only if nothing sits in its lane. Tap a blocked arrow and it turns red, then leaves on its own once the arrow in its way is gone.

- Endless generated levels, with picture-shaped boards mixed in among big rectangles
- A daily puzzle with streaks, a timer and a stats screen
- Casual, Standard and Expert difficulty
- Pinch to zoom on big boards
- Works offline once it has been opened

## Put it on your iPhone home screen

1. Open the game's GitHub Pages address in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open it from the new Slipway icon. It runs full screen and works without a connection.

Progress and stats are stored on the device in the browser's local storage.

The home-screen app keeps its own progress, separate from Safari. To pick up where you left off elsewhere, open **Settings → Go to level**, type the level number and tap **Play**.
